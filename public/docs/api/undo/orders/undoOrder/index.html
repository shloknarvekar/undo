Orders

Undo a purchase

POST
/api/orders/{orderId}/undo

The Undo button. Must be signed by the mandate's shopper key over undo-request-v1:<orderId>:<sha256 hex of reason>:<sha256 hex of photo bytes, or ->:<requestedAt>. requestedAt must be within 15 minutes of now. Runs the fault check, then routes the money: a void inside the window, or a refund, merchant claim, return or human review after capture. Rate limited per visitor.

Path Parameters

orderId*string

Undo order id.

Formatuuid

Request Body

application/json

Why the shopper is undoing, an optional photo, and their signature.

An Undo, signed by the shopper's device key.

reason*string

What went wrong, in the shopper's words.

Length3 <= length <= 500
photo?string

Optional photo of what arrived, as a base64 JPEG, PNG or WebP data URL (max 4 MB).

Match^data:image/(jpeg|png|webp);base64,[A-Za-z0-9+/=]+$
requestedAt*string

When the shopper pressed Undo. Must be within 15 minutes of the server's clock.

Formatdate-time
signature*string

Base64 Ed25519 signature of the undo signing message.

Length80 <= length <= 100
